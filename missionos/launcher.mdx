The launcher is the way into every application. Swipe up from Home to open it, swipe down to leave.

A rail of category chips runs across the top; the grid below shows the applications in the selected
category. Swipe left or right to change category, tap an icon to open it.

## The User Apps tab

The first seven categories are fixed at build time. The eighth is not: it asks the
[User App Platform](/user-apps) what is installed and builds its grid from the answer.

- Tiles appear as soon as a package is installed — no restart.
- Tapping a tile launches the app inside a dedicated app screen.
- The tab pages when more apps are installed than fit one grid.
- The left-edge back swipe always returns here, whatever the app is doing.

## Disabled tiles

Three tiles are present but deliberately inert — **BLE Scanner**, **Voice Recorder** and
**App Link**. They are dimmed and will not open.

They are shown rather than hidden because the launcher grid is part of the specification: the
applications are designed, and their position in the category is settled. Hiding them would make
the grid reflow when they arrive.

## Status

The launcher is implemented and verified on the development prototype, including the User Apps tab,
paging, and launching an installed package. Per-application status is in the
[screen reference](/missionos/screens).
